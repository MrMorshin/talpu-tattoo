THE STUDIO

Talpu Tattoo isn’t an assembly line. Established in 1999 right on the Krauze Pedestrian Mall, we are one of the most respected and longest-standing studios in the Sharon region. We prioritize artistic integrity, hospital-grade hygiene, and one-on-one attention over generic catalogue tattoos. Bridging 27+ years of heritage with modern fine line and blackwork artistry, we deliver serious ink directly from artist to client.
