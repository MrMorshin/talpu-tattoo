FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

01. How do I book an appointment or consultation?

You can reach out directly via WhatsApp using our consultation wizard or contact buttons. We will review your concept, reference ideas, and placement to map out your custom piece before locking in your session.

02. Do I need to pay a deposit to secure a date?

Yes, a deposit is required to lock in your appointment slot. This goes directly toward the final cost of your tattoo and ensures dedicated time is reserved for your custom design and prep.

03. How should I prepare for my tattoo session?

Get a solid night's rest, eat a substantial meal beforehand, stay hydrated, and avoid alcohol or blood thinners for 24 hours prior. Wear comfortable clothing that allows easy access to the area being tattooed.

04. How does the cover-up and scar camouflage process work?

Our studio specializes in cover-ups and scar integration. During your consultation, our artists analyze your existing ink or scar tissue texture to design a custom high-contrast or textured piece that seamlessly hides the old work.

05. What is the studio's policy on minimum age?

In accordance with Israeli law and studio safety standards, clients must be at least 18 years old with a valid government-issued photo ID (Teudat Zehut or passport) to get tattooed.

06. Can I bring guests with me to my session?

To maintain a clean, focused, and calm working environment for our artists, we kindly ask that you attend your appointment solo, or bring a maximum of one guest.

07. How are tattoo prices calculated?

Pricing depends on the size, complexity, detail level, placement, and estimated time required. Custom pieces and large sleeves are quoted per session or project basis during your consultation.

08. What should I do if I need to reschedule my appointment?

We require at least 48 hours notice if you need to reschedule your session so we can reallocate your artist's preparation time. Last-minute cancellations may result in forfeiture of the deposit.
