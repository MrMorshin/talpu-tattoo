FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

01. How do I book an appointment?

We work strictly by appointment. Reach out directly via WhatsApp using our consultation wizard. We do not do catalog copies—every piece is custom. Send us your concept, references, and estimated size so we can map out your design before locking in your session.

02. Do I need to pay a deposit to secure a date?

Yes, a deposit is required to lock in your appointment slot and cover initial sketch preparation. This deposit is fully deducted from the final cost of your tattoo.

03. How should I prepare for my tattoo session?

Get a solid night's rest, eat a substantial meal beforehand, stay hydrated, and avoid alcohol or blood thinners for 24 hours prior. Wear comfortable clothing that allows easy access to the area being tattooed.

04. How does the cover-up and scar camouflage process work?

Our studio specializes in cover-ups and scar integration, but not every tattoo can be covered with anything. During your consultation, we analyze your existing ink or scar tissue to design a custom, high-contrast composition that seamlessly hides the old work.

05. What is the studio's policy on minimum age?

In strict accordance with Israeli law and our studio's safety standards, clients must be at least 18 years old. You must present a valid physical government-issued ID (Teudat Zehut or passport) on the day of the session.

06. Can I bring guests with me to my session?

To maintain a sterile, focused, and calm working environment for our artists, we require clients to attend their appointment solo, or bring an absolute maximum of one guest.

07. How are tattoo prices calculated?

Pricing is determined after consultation and depends on the size, placement, complexity, and estimated time required. Custom pieces and large sleeves are quoted on a per-session or project basis.

08. What should I do if I need to reschedule?

We require a minimum of 48 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling, so we can reallocate your artist's time. Last-minute cancellations will result in the forfeiture of your deposit.
