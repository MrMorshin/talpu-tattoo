HOW TO PREPARE FOR YOUR SESSION

Simple guidelines to ensure a comfortable session and optimal tattoo healing.

😴

Good Rest

Get a full night's sleep before your appointment. A rested body handles the process much better.

🍽️

Eat a Meal

Have a solid meal 1–2 hours before arriving to keep your blood sugar levels stable.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Drink plenty of water the day before. Well-hydrated skin accepts ink smoothly and heals faster.

🚫

No Alcohol

Avoid alcohol or blood thinners for 24 hours prior to prevent excess bleeding and ink distortion.
