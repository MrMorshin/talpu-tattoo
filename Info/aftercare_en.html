HEALING TIMELINE & AFTERCARE

To maintain ink density and crisp lines, strictly follow our studio's healing protocol.

Phase 1: Sterility & Initial Healing

Keep the medical barrier on for the exact duration recommended by your artist. Wash gently with unscented antibacterial soap, pat dry with a clean, single-use paper towel, and apply a very thin layer of recommended aftercare ointment to prevent infection.

Phase 2: Peeling & Flaking

Your tattoo will start to peel and flake. This is completely normal and part of skin regeneration. Do not pick, scratch, or peel the skin under any circumstances—this pulls the ink out and ruins the work. Keep it lightly moisturized.

Phase 3: Ink Settling & Final Care

The peeling will stop, but the skin may still look slightly shiny as deeper layers finish repairing. Continue using a daily moisturizer and protect your ink with high-SPF sunscreen (SPF 50+) whenever exposed to direct sunlight to prevent fading.
