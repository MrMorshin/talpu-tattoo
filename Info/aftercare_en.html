HEALING TIMELINE & AFTERCARE

Step-by-step interactive guide with visual examples for optimal tattoo healing.

Phase 1: Initial Healing & Protection

Keep the initial bandage on for the exact duration recommended by your artist. Wash gently with mild unscented soap, pat dry with a clean paper towel, and apply a very thin layer of recommended aftercare ointment.

Phase 2: Peeling & Flaking

Your tattoo will start to peel and flake like a mild sunburn. This is completely normal! Do not pick, scratch, or peel the skin. Keep it moisturized with a light, unscented lotion as needed.

Phase 3: Settling In & Final Care

The peeling will stop, but the skin may still look slightly cloudy or shiny as deeper layers finish repairing. Continue using daily moisturizer and protect your ink with high-SPF sunscreen when exposed to direct sunlight.
